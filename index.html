Hi, I'm Lei Ke, a first-year Ph.D. student at The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), supervised by Prof. Fangneng Zhan and Prof. Qifeng Chen.

Prior to that, I obtained my M.Sc. degree (2023–2026) from Tsinghua University, supervised by Professor Yujiu Yang. I received my B.Eng. degree (2019–2023) from Shanghai Jiao Tong University.

I am working on generative models, with a focus on world models, video generation, and diffusion acceleration.
